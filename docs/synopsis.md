# Synopsis

Story mode, for translators and the phase 9 story pass. Built from the movie scripts (`script/fmv/BOGO02-18.tsv`),
the briefings (`GRAP/DOKURO`) and the state notes (`docs/re/state.md`). Names: `glossary.md`.

## Premise (BOGO02, the intro)
Dokurobey, the villains' boss, tells every villain trio of the Time Bokan Series that deep in the Arazon, in the
Tirezon Forest, grows a flower called **Top Billing** (主役の座). Whoever brings it to him gets top billing in the new
villain world he will build; the trios will settle who by racing machine against machine. The heroes (Omotchama,
Gan-chan, Ai-chan and the others) resolve to protect the flower. Tone throughout: slapstick, every trio true to its
own show's catchphrases, Dokurobey ending lines with ", y'hear" (D12).

## Chapters
The player picks one trio; its story is one route. Each route: team movie, eight races, a briefing by Dokurobey
before each, the boss flower movie (BOGO10) before course 7, then the trio's ending movie and the credits.

## Briefings (`GRAP/DOKURO#34-74`)
Before each course Dokurobey briefs the trio in pieces joined at run time ("First up:" + "<course>, y'hear"); the
join order is set by code (open question, `docs/status.md`). Courses and their puns: `glossary.md` Places.

## Credits
STAFF.STR after each ending (re-lettered in English, D3, D13); 202 lines in `script/credits.tsv`.
